# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters evaluating Ivan Lara's engineering experience and prospective clients evaluating his work, with equal priority (confirmed November 3, 2026).

## Product Purpose

Present Ivan Lara's existing projects, professional experience, and writing, and make it easy to contact him or download his resume.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve all supplied professional facts, project destinations, articles, five public routes and article URLs, resume downloads, print/export completeness, technology filtering and browser history, disclosures, contact copy fallback, SEO, light/dark themes, and analytics event contracts. Keep Nuxt/Vue and existing image assets. No external CMS or deployment is authorized. Add typed content models, replaceable mock repositories, application queries and framework adapters; source migration should not require UI changes.

## Brand Commitments

Ivan Lara. The user requests a fresh modern, clean, somewhat minimalist Apple-inspired interface with blue as primary color. The incumbent visual design may be replaced; content must remain unchanged.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing projects in src/data/projects.ts, articles in src/data/posts.ts, source-backed resume in src/content/resume.json, prepared WebP previews, supplied PDF/text downloads, and existing CMS schema/export adapters. Do not add unverified employers, achievements, metrics, availability claims, or testimonials.

## Product Principles

Make the work easy to evaluate. Keep factual content source-backed. Make navigation and contact predictable. Separate content acquisition from presentation. Preserve accessible, responsive, prerendered output.
