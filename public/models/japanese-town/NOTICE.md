This directory contains a prepared subset integrated into the portfolio application, not an asset-pack offering. The raw archive and original individual model files are not included.

Modifications: the creator's five building models are combined into eleven orientation variants. Full authored mesh transforms and east/west frontage rotations are baked; complete model bounds, including porches and eaves, are centered in X/Z, based at Y=0 and uniformly normalized to width=1. Source primitive topology, normals and linear material colors are retained. Material colors are baked to vertex colors; no replacement building geometry or source textures are used.

Preparation and source/output hashes: `scripts/prepare-japanese-buildings.mjs` and `src/experience/globe/assets/japanese-town.json` in the application repository.
