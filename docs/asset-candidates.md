# Additional city asset review

Reviewed from the creators' live Sketchfab/itch.io pages on November 5, 2026. No account login or purchase was performed. The cliffs were downloaded from their creator's official page; the other packs were assessed as candidates. Temporary review tabs were used separately from the local preview.

| Supplied candidate | Creator and published complexity | License and current access | Decision for this expansion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Layered Cliffs](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-layered-cliffs-eda6c468f54b421ca300db6fd717c66c/) | SimplePolygon; 13 modular rocks, about 2,800 triangles in the source pack. | Sketchfab lists CC BY 4.0. The [official creator download](https://simplepolygon.itch.io/layered-cliffs) permits personal/commercial end projects with credit, excluding standalone redistribution or resale. | Used three source meshes (476 triangles) as a prepared, recoloured coastal subset. Thirteen placements total 2,044 triangles in one draw. Texture and FBX parsing are excluded from the runtime. Visible credit is in Settings → World credits. |
| [Simple House Pack](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-simple-house-pack-asset-pack-a6410823481348d3b4ac1bb3b198adf0) | SimplePolygon; seven houses, 13,090 building triangles in the downloaded FBX. | Downloaded from the [official house pack page](https://simplepolygon.itch.io/simple-houses-lowpoly-pack), which permits credited personal/commercial end-project use and prohibits standalone redistribution/resale. | **Now used as actual geometry:** all 24 village and Little City façades use the imported pack. A prepared 848,540-byte GLB retains all seven building meshes, with uniform proportions and replacement vertex colours. Shared fitted dimensions drive collisions/minimap; source surfaces participate in transparency. Café and bookshop have working interiors. See [acquisition and preparation](city-model-sources.md). |
| [Cartoon New York City Kitbash Kit promo](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cartoon-new-york-city-kitbash-kit-promo-d0eaea60624f4096925883a0d2574eca) — supplied under “Trees” | Jungle Jim; 3.5 million triangles, 1.8 million vertices. A full urban kit preview with buildings and vehicles, rather than a small standalone tree pack. | The model page lists [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and a free download. Download opens a Sketchfab login prompt. The creator separately links a larger paid kit. | Retain as visual reference. Importing the complete scene would add substantial geometry for a few trees. Reuse would require a deliberately selected, optimized subset and attribution; no download was attempted past login. |
| [K-Street Kit: Alley — demo street](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/k-street-kit-alley-demo-street-05d122fe3f2240b880d73f5888c32853) | haroong; 426,500 triangles, 259,500 vertices. Modern Korean back-alley scene, flat colours and a shared 256² palette. | The Sketchfab description explicitly identifies this as preview-only with download disabled. The [creator's full pack page](https://haroong-7710.itch.io/k-street-kit-alley) describes commercial royalty-free use, no credit requirement, and restrictions on reselling/redistributing assets or training AI. Full text is inside the archive. | Useful reference for dense street composition, storefront details and small props. Do not import the demo scene. The distinct Korean setting and restricted asset redistribution also need deliberate treatment before a public-repository integration. |
| [City Game ready free](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/city-game-ready-free-9095f8bcff1e4c9088ea98a306d5887d) | CG Lads; 21,500 triangles, 12,400 vertices. Modular cartoon storefronts, roofs, balconies and street props. | The page lists [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and a free download. Download opens a Sketchfab login prompt. | Best visual and published geometry fit among these three for a future imported town kit. Keep shortlisted, with attribution required if used. Not added in this pass because the download needs an account and the current expansion already preserves coherent original geometry. |

The official [K-Street free sample](https://haroong-7710.itch.io/k-street-kit-alley-free-sample) is separately offered as a 6.5 MB, name-your-own-price ZIP (v1.0.2): one snack-bar building, eight props, five street tiles and a corner scene, with GLB/FBX/OBJ and editable Blender files. Its page says it uses the full pack's license. It remains an option for a later, narrowly scoped import after reviewing the included LICENSE.txt; it was not downloaded here.

Published triangle counts describe these supplied scenes, not measured runtime performance. Style-fit decisions above are the implementation team's assessment rather than creator claims.
