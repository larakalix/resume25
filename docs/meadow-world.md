## Visual and runtime rules

The scene uses soft stylized PBR geometry with cream walls, blue/coral/violet roofs and a green meadow. `water.ts` provides jade-to-blue depth color, shore-damped waves with analytic lighting normals, broken foam, subtle caustic-like accents, fine duck wakes and the feeding ripple. Its grid matches the ground's spherical grid for a cleaner shoreline; Standard material lighting receives dock/animal shadows without transmission, clearcoat or a reflection render pass. It remains one surface and uses no bitmap textures.

`meadow.ts` provides two curved ribbons per tuft (ten triangles versus the previous nine), with root-fixed, spatially coherent gusts, proximity bending and soft two-sided lighting. Instance colors explicitly convert authored sRGB values to linear working colors. Shared continuous noise gives ground and grass coherent green patches. Most tufts are allocated around the explorable meadow, with shorter, sparser transitions beside paths/banks and the same quality-tier instance caps. Tiny flowers use an independent seed so future grass placement changes do not move them. Instanced clover and butterflies, shore pebbles, shutters and flowerboxes remain. Trees use shared canopy instances; static architecture retains its vertex-colored batches and selective occlusion fading. GSAP owns the deliberate globe-to-follow transition and advances on the same active scene clock.

`building-geometry.ts` provides matching pitched roofs and closed gable infill so the wall reaches the roof without an open strip. Explicit two-floor variants align their window columns and floor bands while keeping entrances at pedestrian height. Sampled foundations overlap the walls and extend into the curved terrain. The six city facades share these parts with the village buildings and retain their night windows and selective see-through behavior.

`building-footprint.ts` projects actual surface points into each building's tangent frame.
