## Geometry and original appearance

The source is a self-contained GLB 2 file generated by obj2gltf, with three identity-transform nodes, three triangle meshes, three materials and one embedded PNG. There are no animations, skins or required extensions. The source is already +Y up: its base is Y=0, the tower's vertical extent is 8.5733890533, and the glass/light meshes occupy the upper tower.

| Runtime node | Original mesh | Vertices | Triangles | Material |

| `lighthouse_light` | `lighthouse_light_1` | 103 | 156 | `LightSG` |

Total: **1,830 triangles**. The source glass material is authored as opaque gray; its name alone is not evidence of transparent glass. The main model keeps its original base-color texture and the lamp keeps its original material factor `[0.84, 0.6, 0.6, 1]`. All three materials are nonmetallic with roughness 1 and zero authored emissive factor.

The original embedded PNG remains **1024×1024**, **279,852 bytes**, with SHA-256 `8379861579edb5ded4d05e330b515eff258612fcdeab5b1a712f689e3b96fc7c`. Its byte sequence is unchanged, as are UVs, normals, source indices, texture references and sampler settings.

## Reproducible preparation

Run:

```sh
node scripts/prepare-lighthouse.mjs '/path/to/Lighthouse by CG ART Creation - c1-hm3Zs-8U.glb'
```

The script checks the exact source byte count and hash before reading the GLB. It validates the header/chunks, self-contained image storage, identity transforms, +Y-up tower shape, triangle topology, accessor ranges and finite coordinates. It modifies only FLOAT POSITION bytes, and recomputes their accessor minimum/maximum values. Normals need no transform because the change is translation plus positive uniform scale.

Full source bounds:

- Minimum: `[-2.0920569897, 0, -1.7228100300]`.
- Maximum: `[1.7228089571, 8.5733890533, 1.7228089571]`.
- Translation center: `[-0.1846240163, 0, -0.0000005364]`.
- Uniform scale: `1 / 8.5733890533`.

The original source extends asymmetrically in X; centering uses the complete geometry bounds rather than assuming the lamp's axis is the bounding center. The prepared full bounds are based at Y=0, centered in X/Z and uniformly normalized to height 1:

- Width: `0.4449659288`.
- Height: `1`.
- Depth: `0.4018969536`.
- Maximum horizontal vertex radius: `0.2289804814`.
- Lamp-mesh bounds center: `[0.0215345437, 0.8114892542, 0.0000000624]`.

The radius is measured from the normalized complete vertex array, not inferred from half the width. A runtime height of 8 meters therefore occupies about 1.832 meters of horizontal radius. Placement/collision should use the fitted actual bounds.

The preparer checks every non-POSITION binary byte against the source, verifies the PNG hash, reloads the resulting GLB structure, and validates finite normalized bounds and source proportions. It preserves all materials, textures and samplers and embeds attribution in the GLB header metadata. It adds no runtime dependency and performs no network requests.

## World integration

The prepared model is uniformly fitted to a 1.8-meter horizontal radius at the outer Bluebird waterfront. The original texture and topology remain intact. Runtime materials make the glass translucent and the beacon emissive after dark; an original bounded light cone rotates slowly and holds still in Calm mode. A sampled stone foundation seats the model above the uneven coast. The shared circular footprint protects the complete source envelope, and actual source surfaces participate in BVH camera transparency. A southern path, Places entry and minimap marker connect it to the city. Runtime ownership releases the three source geometries, three materials and decoded PNG once after occlusion materials are restored.
